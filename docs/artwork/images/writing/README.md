# Writing article artwork

Publisher article images retrieved on 9 September 2026 for the Writing portfolio. Optimised as WebP (maximum 1600 × 1200, no upscaling). Source URLs and crop positions are recorded alongside each article in `src/data/articlesData.js`.

The IPA panel uses the article’s original megaphone image rather than its bordered social preview. All 28 articles have image panels. The three New Digital Age articles share their publisher’s original portrait asset (`article-18.webp`). Decision Marketing’s portrait is retained at its original 414 × 293 resolution.
