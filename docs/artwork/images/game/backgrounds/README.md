### River at sunset

**Initial prompt**

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original standalone background illustration for Sean Betts's GTA VI-inspired portfolio website. This is a reusable environment backdrop behind real event photographs and product screenshots, not a finished poster or website mockup. Landscape 16:9, highest useful native resolution. Scene: London-inspired broad river at sunset, distant modest skyline with a subtle Westminster-like silhouette and a low bridge, open sky, calm water and understated graphic reflections. Match the BACKGROUND visual language described in the supplied GTA VI promotional references: simplified crisp architectural silhouettes, broad flat cel-shaded colour planes, reduced fine detail, pastel atmospheric depth, subtle smooth sky colour transitions; the environment intentionally has restrained contrast so real photographs layered over it will dominate. Palette: pale apricot/peach horizon, dusty pink sky, lavender and muted blue-violet architecture and water. Elegant editorial illustration with precise shapes, not detailed painterly concept art, not photorealistic, no heavy black outlines. Complete coherent edge-to-edge landscape with quiet visual areas distributed across it, distant skyline around the middle-lower portion and generous sky and river. Gentle visual interest at sides, but no strong central focal object and no large dark foreground silhouettes. No foreground people or characters, no faces, no devices, no desks or stationery, no palm trees, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no frames or panel dividers, no baked-in UI overlays or dark legibility gradient. Do not include the photos or product screenshots: output ONLY the clean river background.

**Refinement prompt**

Edit Image 1, the generated London river background. Images 2 and 3 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: study their BACKGROUNDS, not their foreground characters. Keep Image 1's landscape river setting and peach/pink/lavender palette, but dramatically simplify the rendering to match the reference BACKGROUNDS. Use flat, crisp, screenprinted colour shapes: distant buildings should have only two or three tonal planes and minimal windows, trees should be broad silhouettes, water should have a few broad stylized reflection shapes rather than dozens of fine ripples, sky should be a simple pastel gradient with only a few graphic cloud shapes. Reduce local contrast across skyline and river. No atmospheric realistic lighting, no painterly texture, no finely articulated architectural filigree. Westminster should be a simplified lavender silhouette; remove boats, tiny streetlights, cranes and other tiny detail. Keep the open sky and river, and subtle low bridge. The goal is a restrained decorative supporting background for real photographs placed over it, with the same flattened pastel background treatment as the supplied GTA VI references. Environment only, NO people, characters, weapons, lettering, logos, devices, borders, UI or foreground objects. Full image 16:9 rectangle, no montage. Preserve the empty space. Output only the revised background.
